Taskora – Design Choices and Challenges

Design Choices

I wanted Taskora to have a clean and simple design that feels comfortable to use. I chose a light background with dark text and soft green accents to keep the website easy on the eyes.

I kept the layout spacious and avoided adding too many unnecessary elements. Each section has a clear purpose, starting with an introduction to Taskora, followed by its features, how it works, pricing plans and a contact form.

I also added subtle animations and hover effects to make the website feel more interactive without making it distracting.

For the pricing section, I included two plans: Free and Pro, so visitors can easily understand the available options.

Challenges Faced

One of the main challenges was making the website look good on both desktop and mobile screens. Some sections needed adjustments in spacing, layout and text size to work properly on smaller screens.

I also worked on making the navigation links and buttons useful instead of leaving them as static design elements. The contact form was another part I focused on, adding basic validation and a success message after submission.

I used reusable React components to keep the code organised and make changes easier.
